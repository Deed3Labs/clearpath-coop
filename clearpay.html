What is Clear Pay

Clear Pay takes the $1,850 you already send your landlord every month and puts it to work. On-time payments report to all 3 credit bureaus, automatically. Every dollar you save — up to $1,500 a month — earns a 1:1 match in equity credits toward your Clear Deed. And 100% of your savings stays liquid, redeemable to cash, always.

Inside the app

Everything your money does.

01
Accounts

Cash, Savings & linked banks

One balance for spending, one for savings — held as redeemable CLRUSD — and your outside banks connected alongside. See your total, switch between accounts, and watch your equity credits grow, all from the home screen.

02
Pay & Send

Rent, bills & people

Pay rent to route it through the credit flywheel, cover utilities and bills, and send or request money instantly. Every payment is one tap — and the on-time ones do double duty building your file.

03
Card & Credit

Clear Card + earned credit lines

Spend anywhere with the Clear Card (physical + virtual), freeze it in a tap, and unlock Clear Credit lines backed by your savings and behavior — fair terms, transparent limits, no debt trap.
