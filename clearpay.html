Inside the app

Everything your money does, in one place.

Clear Pay feels like the wallet you already know — clean, fast, and mobile-first — with the cooperative's flywheel built quietly underneath every screen.

A
Accounts

Cash, Savings & linked banks

One balance for spending, one for savings (held as redeemable CLRUSD), and your outside banks connected alongside. See your total, switch between accounts, and watch your equity credits grow — all from the home screen.

B
Pay & Send

Rent, bills & people

Pay rent to route it through the credit flywheel, cover utilities and bills, and send or request money instantly. Every payment is one tap — and the on-time ones do double duty building your file.

C
Card & Credit

Clear Card + earned credit lines

Spend anywhere with the Clear Card (physical + virtual), freeze it in a tap, and unlock Clear Credit lines backed by your savings and behavior — fair terms, transparent limits, no debt trap.

Under the hood

The Clear ledger

Behind the familiar interface, every dollar is tracked on a transparent cooperative ledger — one system that holds your money, matches your progress, protects what's yours, and keeps the rules the same for everyone.

Holds
  • Cash balance (USDC)
  • Savings as redeemable CLRUSD
  • Linked outside bank accounts
  • Your Clear Card & credit line
Matches
  • On-time rent → 3-bureau reporting
  • Savings → 1:1 equity credits
  • Up to $1,500 / month matched
  • Progress toward Clear Deed
Protects
  • Savings always redeemable to cash
  • Clear Assurance covers rent in a crisis
  • No hidden fees or interest
Governs
  • One member, one vote
  • Equity credits earmarked for your Deed
  • Fixed, transparent, member-owned rules

Getting started

From download to debt-free.

01

Create your Clear Pay account

Download the app and open your account in minutes. A quick identity check keeps every member of the cooperative safe — then your Cash, Savings, and Card are ready to go.

Free · 5 min
02

Route your rent

Connect your lease and let Clear Pay handle the monthly transfer to your landlord. From your very first on-time payment, we report to all three credit bureaus — automatically.

Key step
03

Save & get matched

Move money into Clear Savings — held as redeemable CLRUSD — and start earning a 1:1 match in equity credits, up to $1,500 a month. Your balance stays 100% liquid the whole time.

Key step

Equity credits and savings compound toward your ownership milestone. Reach it and your Clear Deed unlocks — a debt-free home title, earned through consistency instead of a 30-year loan.
