How it works

Pay rent. own your home.

Every dollar you route through ClearPath does three jobs at once — pays your landlord, builds your ownership stake, and strengthens the safety net for every member.

01

Route your rent through Clear Pay

Same lease, same landlord. ClearPath handles the transfer and reports on-time payments to all three credit bureaus — automatically, for free.

02

Earn equity credits every month

Every on-time payment earns credits regardless of rent size. A perfect 6-month streak unlocks a milestone bonus automatically.

03

Build savings in Clear Equity

Deposit savings and the co-op matches them 1:1 in credits — up to $1,500/month. Your cash stays liquid and 100% yours, always.

04

Cross the threshold. Own your home.

When credits and savings hit the milestone, Clear Deed unlocks. A physical property title — no mortgage, no bank, no debt.
